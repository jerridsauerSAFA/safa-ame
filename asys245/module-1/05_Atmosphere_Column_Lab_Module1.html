Simplified model for teaching. Column values follow the International Standard Atmosphere (sea level 15 °C, 1013.25 hPa, 1.225 kg/m³, lapse rate 1.98 °C per 1,000 ft up to 36,000 ft). Density altitude and the performance bars are approximations — use the aircraft's performance charts for real planning. Sources: ICAO Doc 7488 Manual of the ICAO Standard Atmosphere; Transport Canada Aeronautical Information Manual (TP 14371), MET; FAA Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge (FAA-H-8083-25C), ch. 4 and 11; SAIT ASYS 245 Module 1 Atmosphere.
