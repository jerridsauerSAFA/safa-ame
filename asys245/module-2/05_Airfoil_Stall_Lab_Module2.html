Simplified model for teaching. The CL curve, stall angle (≈ 16°) and centre-of-pressure travel are typical values for a cambered training-aircraft airfoil, not numbers for a specific aircraft — confirm real figures against the aircraft manual. Sources: FAA Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge (FAA-H-8083-25C), ch. 4–5; NASA Glenn Research Center, "Incorrect Lift Theory" (why air over the top is faster is not because it has farther to go); SAIT ASYS 245 Module 2 Lift & Drag; From the Ground Up, Theory of Flight.
