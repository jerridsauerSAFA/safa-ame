How the controls are grouped

Primary flight controls are the ailerons, elevator and rudder. Secondary flight controls are wing flaps, leading-edge devices, spoilers and trim systems. The 172 has no leading-edge devices or spoilers, so its secondary controls are the flaps and the trim tabs.

About this model

A simplified teaching model built from a photo of a 172. Shapes and proportions are approximate. Details such as flap travel and engine rating vary by model year, so check the Pilot's Operating Handbook for a specific aircraft.
