Three practice exams written the way SAIT writes theirs. Each one finishes with your score, a chart of where your marks went, and the exact sections of the notes to study next. Saves on this device.

How SAIT exams work
  • Choose the best answer. Four options, one mark each.
  • About one minute per question: 30 questions, 30 minutes.
  • Watch for All the above, “List …” questions and a matching block.
  • Definitions, cause → effect and reading a figure come up the most.
