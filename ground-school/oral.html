How it works

Four steps per question

  1. Hear the question. Tap 🔊 to hear it again.
  2. Answer out loud, the way you would on the radio.
  3. Check what you said: ✓ said it · ✗ missed it.
  4. Hear the model answer, then mark yourself Right or Wrong, the way the examiner will.

Privacy: nothing is recorded unless you choose Speak to the trainer, and even then the recording stays on this device. Your score saves on this device only.
