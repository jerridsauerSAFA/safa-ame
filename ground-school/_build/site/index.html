How to study each partSame five steps for every part of both exams.

  1. OverviewSee what the part covers.
  2. Cornell NotesRead, then cover the notes and answer the cue questions.
  3. FlashcardsKeep going until every card is mastered.
  4. Practice QuizzesAim to beat the pass mark on both quizzes.
  5. Skills LabPractise it hands-on.

Then the mock exams. Save them until you've finished every part. They tell you if you're ready.
