About the PSTAR

PSTAR (Pre-Solo Test of Air Regulations) is the written exam you pass before a Student Pilot Permit. Its questions come from Transport Canada's free study guide, TP 11919. Use it to check yourself, and use these pages to understand the rules behind the answers.

How to study each lesson
